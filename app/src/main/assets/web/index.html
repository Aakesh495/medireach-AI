NLEM ESSENTIAL MEDICINE

Paracetamol 500mg / 650mg

Jan Aushadhi: ~₹9 for 15 tabs (vs ₹32 branded)

First-line antipyretic for fever and mild pain. Space doses by 4–6 hours; never exceed 4,000mg/day across all tablets.

⚠️ Safe in suspected Dengue fever unlike Aspirin/Ibuprofen/Diclofenac (which increase bleeding risk).

⚠️ Avoid sugary commercial fruit juices for acute diarrhea.

Offline Healthcare Mode — Zero-Internet Emergency First-Aid

OFFLINE PROTOCOL • SNAKEBITE

Snakebite Golden-Hour Protocol

✅ Immobilize bitten limb like a fracture, keep patient calm, remove rings/bangles, and call 108 for immediate transfer to nearest ASV-equipped CHC/District Hospital.

❌ Do NOT tie tight tourniquets, cut/suck the wound, or delay transfer visiting traditional healers.

OFFLINE PROTOCOL • ORS & DEHYDRATION

Home ORS Preparation (WHO Formula)

✅ Dissolve 1 WHO ORS packet in 1 Litre of clean water. If unavailable: mix 6 level teaspoons sugar + 1/2 level teaspoon salt in 1 Litre (5 cups) of clean boiled & cooled water.

🚨 Call 108/104 if child has sunken eyes, lethargy, or no urine for 6+ hours.
